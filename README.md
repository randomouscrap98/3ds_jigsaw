# 3DS Jigsaw

Jigsaw puzzles on the 3DS, made from your own pictures. Pick a picture, choose 50 to 500
pieces and a cut style (classic, irregular, wild, or a random non-grid cut), optionally
with whimsy pieces (hearts, stars, flowers and clouds cut into the puzzle), and put it
together on the touch screen, with your own music playing if you like. A dozen paintings
come with it to get you started.

## Installing

Install `jigsaw.cia` with FBI, or put `jigsaw.3dsx` in `sdmc:/3ds/` and run it from the
Homebrew Launcher.

Sound and music need the DSP firmware dumped to `sdmc:/3ds/dspfirm.cdc` (run DSP1 once if
you haven't). Without it the game just runs silently.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Stylus on a piece | Drag it (and anything joined to it) |
| L or R while dragging | Rotate clockwise |
| Stylus on the table, Circle Pad | Move around |
| D-Pad Up/Down, or X/B | Zoom in/out |
| C-Stick (New 3DS) | Smooth zoom |
| Hold A or D-Pad Left + drag the stylus | Select every piece in a box |
| Hold A or D-Pad Left + tap a piece | Add it to, or remove it from, the selection |
| Stylus on a selected piece | Move the whole selection (L/R turns it as one) |
| Hold Y | Show your time, progress, CPU use and the song playing |
| Y + X | Next song |
| Y + A | Music: off, in order, shuffled (saved with the puzzle) |
| Y + SELECT | Squish the selection into a tidy block (it stays selected) |
| SELECT | Change felt (saved with puzzle) |
| START | Save and go back to the menu |

While Y is held, the other buttons do nothing except D-Pad zoom, so you can't rotate or
leave by accident. The stylus and sticks still work.

A moved selection never snaps to anything; it goes exactly where you put it. Touch the
table or any unselected piece to clear the selection.

Pieces snap together when you drop them close to a matching neighbour with the same
rotation. The puzzle is done once every piece is joined, wherever it ends up on the table.

Your progress saves automatically, and when you press START, go to the HOME menu, or
close the lid.

## Music

Copy MP3, FLAC or Ogg Vorbis files into `sdmc:/3ds/jigsaw/music/` (created on first
launch). Music plays while a puzzle is open and fades out when you go back to the menu.
Each new puzzle has a Music option (Off, In order, Shuffled; In order by default), and it's
saved with the puzzle. Songs are streamed from the SD card, so long files are fine.

Decoding takes a little CPU (hold Y to see how much). If a puzzle ever feels slow, try it
with the music off.

## Adding pictures

The easy way: pick **+ Add pictures over Wi-Fi** at the top of the puzzle list. With the 3DS
on Wi-Fi, open the address it shows (or scan the QR code) in a browser on a phone or
computer on the same network, choose pictures (you can change their names) and press Send. The page shrinks them to
1024 pixels and turns them into JPGs before sending, and they show up in the list when you
press B. Only one picture travels at a time; anyone on your network could reach the page
while that screen is open.

To rename a picture later, pick it and choose **Rename picture**; its saved game and progress
come along.

Black and white pictures or line art? Set **Overlay** to **Hologram** when you start the
puzzle: a soft color wash across the picture (blue, pink, green and yellow corners) gives
every area its own tint, so pieces are easier to sort. Light and dark stay where they were.

Or copy JPG, PNG or BMP files into `sdmc:/3ds/jigsaw/puzzles/` yourself. The first time you start a
puzzle it gets cut and cached, which takes a few seconds; after that it loads quickly.

- The game only uses up to 1024 pixels on the long side, so anything bigger is wasted space
  and loading time.
- Pictures bigger than about 2100×2100 are refused. Big PNGs can still run out of memory
  on an Old 3DS.
- Phone photos may come out sideways, since the game ignores the EXIF rotation.

The resize scripts in `tools/` fix all of that on your PC: they shrink every picture in a
folder to 1024 pixels, apply any rotation, and put the results in a `resized` folder
inside it.

- Windows: drag the folder onto `resize_images.bat` (keep `resize_images.ps1` next to it).
- Linux/macOS: `tools/resize_images.sh ~/Pictures/puzzles` (needs ImageMagick, libvips or
  ffmpeg).

The bundled paintings are copied to the puzzles folder on first launch. Delete any you
don't want; they won't come back.

## Building

Needs devkitPro with `3ds-dev`. Then `make` builds the `.3dsx`.

For the `.cia` (`make cia`), also get
[bannertool](https://github.com/Epicpkmn11/bannertool/releases) and
[makerom](https://github.com/3DSGuy/Project_CTR/releases) and put both in
`/opt/devkitpro/tools/bin`.

Tests for the platform independent code run on your PC with `make -f Makefile.host`
(plain gcc). The icon and banner are generated by `tools/makeart.c`.
