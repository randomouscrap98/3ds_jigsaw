Add to 3DS Jigsaw

Pictures

Pictures are shrunk to 1024 pixels on the long side before they're sent.

Your browser is blocking the picture shrinker (canvas access), so pictures would arrive ruined. Allow canvas access for this page (look for an icon in the address bar), then press Check again.
Or send the files as they are. That only works for pictures that are already small (about 1024 pixels on the long side); bigger ones are refused by the 3DS.

Music

MP3, FLAC or Ogg, sent as they are (roughly 5 seconds per MB). They play during puzzles when music is on.
